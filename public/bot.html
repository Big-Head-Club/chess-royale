How it goes

You get three moves a turn. Runners move up to two squares forward or sideways, never backward, and take one square forward or diagonally forward.

Chests

Step a runner onto a chest and it becomes that piece. Every chest is face-up from the first move. A chest on a ring that is about to fall goes down with it.

  • ♜Rook. The best prize, and both fall at the end of round 7.
  • ♞Knight. Stays to the end.
  • ♝Bishop. Cheap, close, safe.

The board falls

The outer ring is deleted at the end of rounds 4 and 7. Anything standing on it is gone. Take everything to win, or hold the most when round 11 ends.
